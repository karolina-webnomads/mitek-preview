Adapting to Right to Work laws & legislation changes

Following recent laws and legislative changes to Right to Work identity screening, UK employers now have the option of performing Right to Work checks remotely using digital identity service providers (IDSPs).

On October 1st 2022, the temporary remote screening measures put in place during the pandemic came to an end. All employers will now need to choose one of the following three ways to verify the identity of your applicants:

  • A hard copy, manual Right to Work identity check
  • A digital Right to Work check via an IDSP - this service is open to those with British or Irish passports, or an Irish passport card
  • A Home Office online Right to Work check - this service is only open to overseas nationals with a biometric residence card or permit

A certified Digital Identity Service Provider

HooYu, a Mitek company, has been certified against standards set in the UK government Digital Identity and Attributes Trust Framework (DIATF) to provide digital identity screening for Right to Work, Right to Rent and criminal record (DBS) checks. That means all areas of our governance, systems, controls and technology have been audited and reviewed and found to be robust, effective and safe.

Frequently asked questions about Mitek Verified Identity Platform

MiVIP is built for organizations that need to verify identities, reduce fraud, meet compliance requirements, and deliver secure digital experiences at scale.

Banks, fintechs, lenders, marketplaces, and regulated enterprises can use MiVIP to create identity journeys that match their risk policies, customer expectations, and compliance obligations.

Whether teams need to onboard new customers, authenticate returning users, screen for financial crime, or protect high-risk transactions, MiVIP gives them the flexibility to apply the right identity services at the right moment.

Most organizations running identity verification today rely on a combination of separate tools: one for document checks, another for biometrics, a third for AML screening, and something else for authentication. Each tool works in isolation. The gaps between them are where problems develop.

MiVIP helps businesses move beyond fragmented identity tools and manage the identity lifecycle through a more connected platform.

When identity tools do not share data or logic, several problems follow:

  • Slower fraud response. A fraud signal detected in one system does not automatically inform decisions in another. By the time the information is acted on, the attack may have succeeded.
  • Inconsistent customer experiences. Separate tools mean separate user interfaces, separate friction points, and separate failure modes. Customers notice.
  • Higher operational overhead. Managing vendor relationships, contracts, and integrations for each tool takes time and resource that could go toward building better products.
  • Compliance gaps. When verification, screening, and authentication are not connected, audit trails become fragmented and compliance reporting becomes harder to defend.

MiVIP connects verification, fraud defense, compliance screening, and authentication so that each layer informs the others. A document check feeds into a biometric match. A risk score influences whether AML screening is triggered. An onboarding biometric becomes the credential for future authentication. The result is a more consistent, defensible identity process, managed through a single platform rather than across multiple vendor relationships.

For regulated organizations, that consistency matters. Fraudsters increasingly use AI to create synthetic identities and convincing fake documents. A 456% increase in GenAI-enabled scams was recorded in 2025. Responding to that threat requires defenses that are coordinated, not siloed.

Identity verification is the process of ensuring a person is who they are claiming to be. It’s a crucial part of doing business, and is essential for preventing fraud, financial crime, and complying with legal and regulatory requirements. At a time when so many transactions take place online, identity verification plays a crucial role in building digital trust, and can be accomplished using a variety of methods, from checking authoritative data sources, to using biometrics and document verification.

Identity verification services typically work by capturing personal information from an individual (sometimes extracted from a government-issued ID) and comparing it to trusted and authoritative data sources to ensure that a) the person exists and is a real person, b) there is a record of them that has existed over time, and c) the person claiming the identity is the actual owner of it. In addition, most identity verification services will provide other means of risk assessment in order to meet financial crime compliance guidelines (where required).

The banking sector is heavily regulated, with extensive KYC, AML and good practice guidelines (e.g. GPG45 in the UK) in place to protect consumers and businesses against fraud, financial crime and terrorist financing. ‘Banking-grade’ identity verification refers to solutions that meet the rigorous regulatory standards imposed upon the sector. These systems typically involve multi-layered checks that can include document authentication, biometrics, risk assessments and more. The term has come to represent the highest benchmark for identity verification with regards to security, accuracy and compliance. The Mitek MiVIP platform is considered ‘banking-grade’, and the number of banks and financial institutions that can be counted as customers is a testament to that.

Banks and financial institutions choose MiVIP because it helps them meet the rigorous standards expected of them by regulators and consumers alike. It gives them complete flexibility and control of their identity verification solutions. Whether that’s to manage, configure and control a specialist identity solution (like document verification) that’s been integrated via an API into an existing customer journey(s), or to orchestrate layers of digital trust and fraud tools to find that optimal balance between strong fraud protections and simple customer experiences. Mitek’s ability to protect against even the most sophisticated AI fraud, like deepfakes and other manipulated content, plays an important role in meeting the highest regulatory standards.
